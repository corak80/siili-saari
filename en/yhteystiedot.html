When a hedgehog needs help

Call us if you see any of these. If you are unsure, call anyway — we will assess the situation over the phone.

  • A hedgehog is out and about in the middle of the day
  • Visible wounds, blood or fly eggs
  • The hedgehog is lying on its side or staggering
  • The hedgehog is tangled in netting or litter
  • A small juvenile alone, with no mother
  • In autumn, a hedgehog weighing under 600 g

More detailed guidance is in the hedgehog care guide.
